PsyCare — Wireframe Index
Public
Home
Landing page for unauthenticated visitors. Introduces PsyCare, highlights featured approved doctors, and offers entry points to book a doctor or start a login/registration flow.
Find Doctors
Public doctor directory. Lets visitors search and filter approved doctors by name, specialization, or consultation mode before viewing a profile or starting the booking flow.
Patient
Login
Patient sign-in screen. Authenticates against the patient (web) guard; rejects invalid credentials and blocks banned accounts with a suspension message before granting dashboard access.
Register
Patient account creation form. Collects name, email, mobile number, and password, then auto-logs the new patient in immediately — no email verification or approval step required.
Dashboard / Appointments
Patient's home screen after login. Summarizes upcoming, completed, and pending-payment appointment counts, lists upcoming bookings, and links onward to start a new booking.
Booking — Pre-Assessment
Fourth step of the 5-step booking wizard. Optional PHQ-9/GAD-7 screener questions feed a risk analysis that can flag self-harm or immediate escalation; patients may skip it entirely.
Booking — Review & Pay
Final booking step. Shows appointment summary, assessed risk level, and fee breakdown, then reserves the time slot and redirects the patient to Stripe Checkout to complete payment.
AI Companion
Conversational chat with Lumi, the AI companion. Detects self-harm language for an immediate crisis response, replies via Gemini with synthesized voice, and can generate a clinical report on finish.
Therapy Room Session
Live group therapy video call joined via WebRTC signaling over Reverb. Patients appear to others only by an anonymous label, protecting real identity during group sessions.
Mood Tracker
Daily mood logging screen. Patients record a mood score, tags, and an optional note once per day — resubmitting today's entry overwrites it — and view a 30-day mood history chart.
Health Records
Aggregated clinical history view. Combines NLP classification results, generated reports, and past appointments with prescriptions into a single risk-progression dashboard.
Settings & Consent
Account settings page. Edits profile details and password, plus a per-doctor consent toggle controlling which treating doctors can access this patient's health data.
Doctor
Login
Doctor sign-in screen. Authenticates against the doctor guard, then routes the doctor through onboarding or approval-status gates before reaching the dashboard.
Register
Doctor account creation form. Collects name, email, license number, and password, then creates the account in pending-approval status, requiring profile completion and admin review.
Onboarding
Mandatory profile-completion form shown before a doctor can practice. Captures specialization, experience, bio, and photo; first-time completion notifies admins for approval review.
Dashboard
Doctor's home screen, scoped to the currently selected clinic. Shows today/upcoming/completed appointment counts, crisis-flag totals, and today's schedule with per-patient risk levels.
Appointment & Prescription
Single appointment view showing the patient's screener results. Lets the doctor issue or replace a prescription with line items, then mark the appointment completed or cancelled.
Crisis Queue
Safety-critical worklist of patients whose screener responses flagged self-harm risk or required immediate escalation. Doctors acknowledge each case to mark it as reviewed.
Patient NLP Report
AI-generated clinical insight for one patient. Shows a risk-level trend chart, symptom frequency, and conversation history, with a manual sync button for missed classifications.
Therapy Rooms
Group therapy room management. Doctors create rooms with eligible patients, then start, host, or end live sessions, with the ability to remove a disruptive participant mid-call.
Earnings
Gross earnings dashboard covering confirmed and completed appointments. Displays all-time and monthly totals, a 6-month trend chart, and a per-clinic revenue breakdown.
Payouts
Manual payout ledger, not a live payment processor. Shows amounts pending per clinic and lets the doctor acknowledge receipt only after the clinic has marked a batch as paid.
Medical Center
Login
Clinic sign-in screen for the primary medical-center account. Blocks login until admin approval, showing a rejected or pending-review message otherwise.
Register
Clinic registration form. Collects name, registration number, contact details, and password, then submits the application for admin review with no auto-login until approved.
Dashboard
Clinic's home screen. Summarizes active doctor count, pending affiliation requests, today's appointments, and monthly revenue, alongside specialization breakdown and recent activity.
Doctors — Search & Requests
Lets clinics search approved, profile-complete doctors and send affiliation requests. The doctor must accept before joining; existing affiliations and pending requests are tracked separately.
Patients
Clinic-scoped patient directory. Only shows patients who have had at least one appointment at this specific clinic, preventing cross-clinic access to other facilities' patients.
Analytics
Operational analytics for the clinic. Reports month-over-month appointment volume trend, monthly revenue, busiest doctors, and cancellation rate over a 6-month window.
Payments & Payouts
Revenue and payout ledger. Shows succeeded payments and unpaid amounts owed per doctor, and lets the clinic batch-mark a doctor's outstanding payments as paid.
Staff Management
Primary-account-only screen for managing clinic staff logins. Staff are created with a password and can be soft-disabled, which force-logs them out on their next request.
Settings
Clinic configuration screen. Edits profile and contact details, uploads a logo, and sets operating hours and the per-appointment facility fee charged to patients.
Super Admin
Login
Admin sign-in screen. Admin accounts are seeded out-of-band with no self-registration and no status/ban gate — only credential validation stands between login and the dashboard.
Dashboard
Platform-wide overview for administrators. Totals medical centers, doctors, and patients, flags pending approvals, and surfaces doctor specialization breakdown and recent activity.
Medical Center Approvals
Approval queue for clinic registrations. Admins search and filter by status, then approve or reject a clinic — approval is what unlocks that clinic's ability to log in.
Doctor Approvals
Approval queue for doctor accounts. Admins review profile-complete doctors and approve or reject them; doctors who haven't finished onboarding cannot be approved yet.
Patients — Ban / Restore
Platform-wide patient directory with moderation controls. Admins search, filter by active/banned status, and ban or restore an account, which force-logs the patient out immediately.
Platform Reports
Aggregate platform analytics. Shows 6-month growth trends, appointment status and risk-level breakdowns, total revenue, and top-performing centers and doctors.
Mail Check (SMTP)
Diagnostic tool for outbound email. Shows a live SMTP readiness checklist and lets an admin send a test email to verify deliverability without affecting the app's default mailer.
Settings
Admin's personal account settings. A simple form for updating profile details and changing the admin's own login password.
